## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Timing Guidelines
- **Total duration:** 5:45 - 5:50
- **Pace:** Medium tempo, clear enunciation
- **Music:** Subtle, modern background track at -20dB
- **Transitions:** Quick cuts between sections (0.5s max)

### Screen Recording Setup
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 or higher
- **Frame rate:** 60fps for smooth playback
- **Cursor:** Show cursor for actions, hide during playback
- **Zoom:** Use screen zoom for small UI elements

### UI Preparation Before Recording
1. Clear any test projects
2. Prepare restaurant/cafe URLs for demo
3. Pre-load stock media searches to avoid loading delays
4. Disable browser extensions and notifications
5. Set timeline zoom to comfortable viewing level

### Demo Content Suggestions
- **Restaurant URL:** Choose one with good imagery and video
- **Search terms:** "coffee pouring", "cafe ambiance", "food preparation"
- **Music genre:** "Upbeat", "Corporate", or "Ambient"
- **Text examples:** "Grand Opening", "50% Off Today", "Visit Us"

### Key Messages to Emphasize
1. **Speed:** One-click generation vs multi-step wizards
2. **Authenticity:** Real brand content vs AI avatars/slideshows
3. **Power:** Full timeline editor vs basic controls
4. **Intelligence:** AI that enhances vs AI that dominates
5. **Flexibility:** Professional tools without complexity

### Visual Hierarchy During Recording
- **Primary focus:** Timeline and canvas (60% of screen time)
- **Secondary focus:** Side panels (30% of screen time)
- **Tertiary focus:** Properties panel (10% of screen time)

### Troubleshooting
- If generation is slow, mention "processing time varies"
- If preview lags, pause and say "previewing at full quality"
- Keep backup clips ready in case live demo has issues

## VIDEO PRODUCTION WORKFLOW RECOMMENDATIONS

### RECOMMENDED APPROACH: Pre-Record Narration + Screen Recording

**Why this works best:**
1. **Consistency** - You can re-record screen actions without redoing voiceover
2. **Efficiency** - Separate audio and video editing reduces mistakes
3. **Quality** - Focus on each element independently for better results
4. **Flexibility** - Easy to adjust timing and pacing in post-production

### STEP-BY-STEP PRODUCTION PROCESS:

#### Phase 1: Audio Production (30-45 minutes)
1. **Generate AI Narration:**
   - Use ElevenLabs or similar service
   - Copy the paragraph script above
   - Choose a professional, clear voice (avoid overly casual or robotic)
   - Export as high-quality MP3 or WAV (320kbps minimum)

2. **Add Background Music:**
   - Find a subtle, modern corporate/tech track
   - Ensure it's 5:50+ in length
   - Mix music at -20dB to -25dB below narration
   - Use a gentle fade-in (0-3 seconds) and fade-out (last 5 seconds)

#### Phase 2: Screen Recording (1-2 hours)
1. **Record in Segments:**
   - Don't try to record all at once
   - Record each PART as a separate clip
   - Listen to narration with headphones while recording
   - Match your actions to the narration timing
   - If you mess up, just restart that segment

2. **Recommended Segments:**
   - **Segment 1:** Opening + URL Generation (Parts 1)
   - **Segment 2:** Timeline Editing (Part 2)
   - **Segment 3:** Stock Libraries (Part 3)
   - **Segment 4:** AI Features (Part 4)
   - **Segment 5:** Text & Typography (Part 5)
   - **Segment 6:** Transitions (Part 6)
   - **Segment 7:** Visual Effects (Part 7)
   - **Segment 8:** Showing Final Ad (Part 8)
   - **Segment 9:** Export (Part 9)
   - **Segment 10:** Closing (Closing + CTA)

3. **Recording Tips:**
   - Use OBS Studio or similar (free, high quality)
   - Record at 60fps for smooth playback
   - Show cursor during actions, can hide in post if needed
   - Move cursor slowly and deliberately
   - Pause for 2-3 seconds on important UI elements

#### Phase 4: Video Editing (2-3 hours)
1. **Assembly:**
   - Import all segments into DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, or Final Cut
   - Import your narration audio track
   - Lay narration on audio track 1
   - Lay background music on audio track 2

2. **Sync & Trim:**
   - Place video segments to match narration timing
   - Cut out mistakes, loading screens, unnecessary pauses
   - Speed up slow sections (1.2x-1.5x) if needed
   - Add quick cuts (0.3s fade) between major sections
